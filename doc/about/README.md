About: background documents
===========================

This folder holds background PDFs for the block and for the wider curriculum it sits in. Nothing
here is required reading for the two days. It is here so that the context of the block is part of
the repository rather than something that lives in somebody's inbox, and so that anyone picking up
these materials later can see what they were written to fit into.

- [**DABDGE_program_v3.pdf**](DABDGE_program_v3.pdf) - the detailed programme and session plan for
  the curricular unit as a whole: the four-week lifecycle arc, the pedagogical principles, learning
  outcomes, the week-by-week schedule, the six continuous assessment items and the exam, the AI-use
  policy, and the table of hand-offs to the downstream CUs. The two days in this repository are
  week 2 of that plan. If you want to know why something here is kept light, the hand-off table says
  which CU picks it up.

- [**flyer_mbcma.pdf**](flyer_mbcma.pdf) - the programme flyer for the MSc in Molecular and
  Environmental Computational Biology at the University of Minho, hosted by the Department of
  Biology (ECUM) and the Centre of Molecular and Environmental Biology (CBMA) at Campus de Gualtar,
  Braga. Two years, 120 ECTS, and the curriculum in which DABDGE is the first unit of the first
  semester.

Both documents describe the edition of 2026/27. Times, dates and staffing change between editions;
where this repository and a PDF disagree about a practical detail, the repository is the more
recent.
